Daily Golf Trivia

·

Every day you play three holes of golf trivia about golf YouTube and the Internet Invitational: a par 4, a par 3 and a par 5. Every answer is a shot.

How to play

  • Every answer is a shot. Answer right and you find the fairway, hit the green or hole the putt. Answer wrong and it costs a stroke: into the trees, a missed green, a duffed chip or a lipped-out putt.
  • Birdie means every answer right: 2 in a row on the par 3, 3 on the par 4 and 4 on the par 5. Miss the green but get the chip right and you have a putt to get up and down for par.
  • 🎯 Take a risk once per hole. Tap the green to drive it on the par 4 or go for it in two on the par 5, or tap the flag on the par 3. You answer one question more than the safe way needs, all for one shot: 3 in a row on the par 4 and par 5, 2 on the par 3. All right and you save a stroke. On the par 3 you stop it inches from the pin with a hint on the birdie putt, and 1 time in 20 it drops for a hole in one. Miss one and it splashes into the water: penalty stroke and a drop next to the water.
  • You keep playing until the ball is in the hole, but you pick up at 4 over par.

After the round you get a scorecard you can share. Play every day to keep your streak going, or play a practice round with new questions.

A new round starts at midnight, and questions do not repeat for weeks. Want more? Play Guess the YouTube Golfer, the Internet Invitational game, the Internet Invitational Draft, or read our golf articles.
