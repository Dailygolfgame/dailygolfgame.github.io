Every day you play nine holes of golf trivia about golf YouTube and the Internet Invitational, par 36. Every answer is a shot.

How to play

  • Every answer is a shot. Answer right and you find the fairway, hit the green or hole the putt. Answer wrong and it costs a stroke: into the trees, a missed green, a duffed chip or a lipped-out putt.
  • Nine holes, par 36: two par 3s, five par 4s and two par 5s. Birdie means every answer right: 2 in a row on a par 3, 3 on a par 4 and 4 on a par 5. Miss the green but get the chip right and you have a putt to get up and down for par.
  • 🎯 Take a risk once per hole. Tap the green to drive it on a par 4 or go for it in two on a par 5, or tap the flag on a par 3. You answer one question more than the safe way needs, all for one shot: 3 in a row on par 4s and par 5s, 2 on par 3s. All right and you save a stroke. On a par 3 you stop it inches from the pin with a hint on the birdie putt, and 1 time in 20 it drops for a hole in one. Miss one and it splashes into the water: penalty stroke and a drop next to the water.
  • You keep playing until the ball is in the hole, but you pick up at 4 over par. Your round is saved, so you can stop between holes and come back later the same day.

The questions are about golf YouTubers, their channels, handicaps and countries, and the Internet Invitational teams. Many are built from our golf YouTuber handicap list and the Internet Invitational player list, so they follow the latest numbers.

A new round starts at midnight. Want more? Play Guess the YouTube Golfer, the Internet Invitational game, the Internet Invitational Draft, or read our golf articles.
