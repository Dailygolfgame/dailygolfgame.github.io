About Dailygolf

Dailygolf makes free daily games for fans of golf YouTube: the creators, their channels, their handicaps and the Internet Invitational.

Golf on YouTube has become a sport of its own, with creators who play matches, chase scratch and now compete for millions of dollars in the Internet Invitational. Dailygolf turns that world into quick daily games. They are free, need no account and work on phones and computers.

The games

  • Guess the YouTube Golfer: a new mystery creator every day. Each guess shows how close you are on country, handicap, subscribers, age and channel.
  • Guess the Internet Invitational Player: find the mystery player from the 2026 field by team and handicap.
  • Internet Invitational Draft: draft your own teams from the 64 players and simulate scramble, alternate shot and singles matches.
  • Daily Nine Trivia: nine holes of golf YouTube trivia, where every answer is a shot.

For tour fans there is also Guess the Pro Golfer.

Why we made it

We watch a lot of golf YouTube, and we love games you can finish in a few minutes over a coffee. Who has the better handicap, Grant Horvat or Micah Morris? Which team was Mac Boucher on? Dailygolf is built around the questions fans already argue about, and a good way to learn something new about the creators you watch.

Where the data comes from

Creator handicaps come from public sources: handicap-tracker.com where a player is listed there, and otherwise fan-made leaderboards and recent scores. Each player page says where its handicap comes from. Subscriber counts come from public channel statistics, and ages are calculated from birth dates. Internet Invitational teams and results are taken from the episodes and checked against several match reports, which we list on our articles.

Handicaps and subscriber counts change all the time, so a clue can be slightly out of date. If you spot a mistake, tell us on the contact page and we will correct it.
