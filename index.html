How to Play Dailygolf

Guess the mystery male professional golfer in 5 guesses or less.

Each guess will reveal clues comparing your player to the mystery golfer:

  • Green: Exact match for that attribute.
  • Yellow: Partial match (Age within ±2 years, Same continent, or PGA Tour wins within ±3).
  • Gray: Incorrect attribute.
  • ▲ / ▼ Arrows: Indicate if the mystery player's age (age at death for late legends), majors, or wins are higher or lower.

Guess the mystery golf YouTuber in 5 guesses or less.

Each guess compares your creator with the mystery creator:

  • Green: Exact match (same country, handicap, subscribers, age or channel).
  • Yellow: Close: handicap within ±1.5, subscribers within 1.5×, age within ±2, or a shared country.
  • Gray: No match.

How to play the Daily Golf Game

Dailygolf is a free daily golf guessing game, like Wordle for golf fans. There are two daily games: Guess the Pro Golfer and Guess the YouTube Golfer. Every day there is one mystery professional golfer. You have five guesses. After each guess you see how close you are: country, continent, tour, age, major championships and PGA Tour wins turn green for an exact match and yellow when you are close. Arrows show whether the mystery golfer's age, majors or wins are higher or lower.

Frequently asked questions

When does a new golfer appear?

A new mystery golfer is available every day. Come back tomorrow to keep your streak going.

Which golfers are in the game?

Current stars from the PGA Tour, LIV Golf and DP World Tour, plus legends such as Jack Nicklaus, Ben Hogan and Arnold Palmer.

What counts as wins?

Wins are official PGA Tour victories, major championships included.

Is Dailygolf free?

Yes. It is free to play in your browser on phone or desktop, with no account needed.
