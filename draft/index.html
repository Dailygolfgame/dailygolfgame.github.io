Scramble Draft

Build a four-player scramble team of golf YouTubers. You get four picks, each from four players. Low handicaps win scrambles, players from the same channel bring chemistry and big channels bring star power. Then see your team rating. The picks are the same for everyone each day.

How the rating works

  • Golf (60%) comes from your team's scramble handicap: 25% of the best player's handicap, 20% of the second, 15% of the third and 10% of the fourth.
  • Chemistry (25%): two players from the same channel, like Good Good or Big Wedge Golf, form a strong link. Two players from the same country form a light link.
  • Star power (15%) grows with the total YouTube subscribers of your team.

Your daily draft earns 🪙 20 to 40 coins depending on the grade. Spend them on gear and club upgrades in Dailygolf Tour. Handicaps and subscribers come from our golf YouTuber handicap list.
