Internet Invitational Draft Simulator

Run the 2026 Internet Invitational draft. Captain one team, two, three or all four (pass the phone to a friend), and AI captains draft the rest. Then a draw decides the semifinals, and you and the other captain take turns sending out pairs for a scramble round and an alt-shot round. The winners meet in the final.

Which teams do you draft?

Tap one or more captains. The AI drafts the teams you leave out.

Snake draft: the pick order reverses every round (Dalke, Bryan, Finch, Ruffels, then back). 60 picks, 15 per team. Handicaps are estimates by YT Golf Stats.

How the simulator works

The four captains are fixed, as in the real event: Brad Dalke, Wesley Bryan, Peter Finch and Ryan Ruffels. AI captains mostly take the best available handicap but sometimes reach for someone else, so no two drafts are the same. When the draft is done, one team is drawn at random and picks its semifinal opponent. Every match is eight two-player pairs against eight: one round of scramble and one of alternate shot, a point per pairing, 16 points in total, with a sudden-death playoff at 8–8. In each round the captains take turns: one team sends out a pair and the other answers with a pair of its own, then they switch roles, until all 16 players are out. The winners meet in the final with the same format. Better pairs win more often, but upsets happen, just like on the course.
