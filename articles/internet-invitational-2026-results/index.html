Internet Invitational 2026: Every Result, the Winners and the $4 Million Prize

By the Dailygolf team · Updated November 13, 2026

The second Internet Invitational doubled its field to 64 golf creators, athletes and personalities and quadrupled the prize to $4 million. After six episodes at Big Cedar Lodge in Missouri, Fat Perez, Riggs and Bobby Ray were the last three standing. Here is every result, from the first scramble to the final putt.

The short version
  • Champions: Fat Perez, Riggs and Bobby Ray
  • Runners-up: Big John, Ryan Whitney and Salim
  • Final score: 5&3 in 18 holes of alternate-shot match play at Payne’s Valley
  • Prize: $4 million, split between the three winners (about $1.33 million each)
  • Aired: six episodes on YouTube, October 26 to November 12, 2026

Schedule and format

The 64 players voted for four captains, who then drafted teams of 16. Day 1 cut the field to two teams, Day 2 cut it to one team, and that team was split up so its own players fought for the title.

The 6–2 morning and 6½–4½ in the singles made it 12½–6½. Team Bryan was out, and all 16 players left in the event came from Team Ruffels.

Episode 5: knockout and semifinal at Payne’s Valley

Team Ruffels was split into four teams of four, captained by Big John, Grant Horvat, Micah Morris and Ryan Ruffels. On the captains’ par 5, Big John and Ruffels made birdie and took their teams through, while Horvat and Morris made par. On the next hole Ryan Whitney made par and Jake Adams made bogey, which sent Team Horvat home: Grant Horvat, Jake Adams, Paige Spiranac and Jon Gruden.

The 12 players left played the semifinal as four teams of three: nine holes of alternate-shot stroke play, with the top two reaching the final.

Salim holed a bogey putt on the 9th to get Team Big John into the final by one shot.

The final: Team Fat Perez beat Team Big John 5&3

The final was 18 holes of 3v3 alternate-shot match play at Payne’s Valley, the Tiger Woods design at Big Cedar Lodge. Fat Perez, Riggs and Bobby Ray led from the 1st hole and were 3 up at the turn. Big John and Ryan Whitney won holes back, but penalty strokes, mostly off the tee, cost Team Big John too much. A par putt from Riggs on the 14th put Team Fat Perez dormie, and the match ended on the 15th: 5&3.

Fat Perez, Riggs and Bobby Ray split the $4 million and became the first winners of the Cody “Beef” Franke Trophy. Riggs also captained one of the two teams on Day 1 of the first Internet Invitational in 2025.
