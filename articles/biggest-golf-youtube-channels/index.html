The Biggest Golf YouTube Channels in 2026

By the Dailygolf team · Updated October 2026

Golf has some of the most popular sports channels on YouTube. Here is a look at the biggest names in October 2026, what they are known for and where to start watching.

Rick Shiels Golf

Rick Shiels, a former PGA professional from England, is the most-subscribed dedicated golf channel in this list. His videos cover club reviews, practice tips and playing lessons, making it a good place to start if you want to improve.

Good Good

Good Good built an audience through friendly competition. Founded by a group of friends including Garrett Clark, Matt Scharff and Stephen Castaneda, the channel features match play episodes, team events and travel videos with a mix of strong amateurs and professionals.

Bryan Bros Golf

Brothers Wesley and George Bryan combine trick shots with matches and fun challenges. Wesley is also a former PGA Tour winner, which makes the quality of the golf in their videos particularly high.

Bryson DeChambeau

Bryson is a major champion on the pro tours who also runs one of the biggest golf channels. His videos give a close look at the equipment and ideas of one of the game's most analytical players.

Big Wedge Golf

Big Wedge Golf is a group channel from the UK where creators best known for other kinds of YouTube content, including Callux, Calfreezy and the Sidemen's Harry, Simon and Josh, play golf together. It is a good example of how golf has spread into the wider creator world.

Where do they rank as golfers?

The biggest channels are not always the best golfers. Fan-made leaderboards rank creators by handicap, and the top places are held by players such as Brad Dalke, with many entertainers further down the list.
