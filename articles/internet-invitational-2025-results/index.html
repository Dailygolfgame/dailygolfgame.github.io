Internet Invitational 2025: How the First Edition Was Won

By the Dailygolf team · Updated November 13, 2026

The first Internet Invitational, in 2025, put 48 golf creators, athletes and internet personalities on the courses at Big Cedar Lodge with $1 million on the line. Over three days the field was cut from 48 to three, and Brad Dalke, Francis Ellis and Cody “Beef” Franke won the final on the 18th hole. Here is how it happened.

The short version
  • Champions: Brad Dalke, Francis Ellis and Cody “Beef” Franke
  • Runners-up: Paige Spiranac, Frankie Borrelli and Malosi Togisala
  • Final score: 1 up in 18 holes of alternate-shot match play at Payne’s Valley
  • Prize: $1 million, split between the three winners
  • Aired: six episodes on YouTube in October and November 2025

Event facts

HostsBarstool Sports and Bob Does Sports, with Dave Portnoy as host and rules official
VenueBig Cedar Lodge, Ridgedale, Missouri
FilmedThree days in August 2025
Field48 players
Prize$1 million to the winning team of three
RecognitionFinalist for Best Video Series at the 2025 Shorty Awards

Day 1: Team Riggs v Team Bob

On the first night the field was drafted into two teams of 24, captained by Riggs of Fore Play and Bob of Bob Does Sports. The 12 pairs per team played a nine-hole scramble session in the morning and an alternate-shot session in the afternoon.

Day 1 also produced the event’s first big talking point: Luke Kwon missed his tee time and Dave Portnoy gave him a four-hole penalty.

Day 2: Team Frankie v Team Riggs

The 24 survivors from Team Riggs were drafted again into two teams of 12, captained by Riggs and Frankie Borrelli, using the same scramble and alternate-shot format. Team Riggs took the morning, but Team Frankie won the afternoon and the day, so its 12 players went through and Riggs’ second team went home.

Day 3: the semifinal

The last 12 players were split into four teams of three for an alternate-shot stroke-play semifinal. The top two teams reached the final.

The final: Dalke, Ellis and Franke win 1 up

The final was 18 holes of 3v3 alternate-shot match play at Payne’s Valley. Brad Dalke, Francis Ellis and Cody Franke were 2 up with three holes to play, but Paige Spiranac, Frankie Borrelli and Malosi Togisala won the 16th and 17th to square the match. On the 18th, Borrelli’s pitch went into the water, and Team Dalke won the hole, the match and the $1 million.

The rules talking points

  • The fescue on the 9th: video showed Paige Spiranac pressing down long grass in front of the ball before her partner’s shot. Her team was told about it a hole later and lost the hole anyway; no further action was taken.
  • The rangefinder: Malosi Togisala was accused of using the slope function on his rangefinder, which was not allowed. The video could not prove it, he denied it, and no penalty was given.
  • The first hole: a short putt was not conceded, which led to words between the teams in the opening minutes of the final.

Remembering Cody “Beef” Franke

Cody Franke died the day before the series was due to air. The final episode opened with a tribute to him, Barstool and the Fore Play podcast set up a scholarship in his name at Ferris State University, and the trophy for the 2026 event was named the Cody “Beef” Franke Trophy.

What came next

Dave Portnoy confirmed the Internet Invitational would be held every year. The 2026 edition grew to 64 players and a $4 million prize, and was won by Fat Perez, Riggs and Bobby Ray. Read every result: Internet Invitational 2026: every result, the winners and the $4 million prize.
