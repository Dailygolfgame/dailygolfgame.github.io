Internet Invitational Draft Simulator

Run the 2026 Internet Invitational draft. Captain one team, two, three or all four (pass the phone to a friend), and AI captains draft the rest. Then see who wins the simulated tournament.

Which teams do you draft?

Tap one or more captains. The AI drafts the teams you leave out.

Snake draft: the pick order reverses every round (Dalke, Bryan, Finch, Ruffels, then back). 60 picks, 15 per team. Handicaps are estimates by YT Golf Stats.

Also try: Guess the Internet Invitational Player · Handicap list · YouTube Golfer

How the simulator works

The four captains are fixed, as in the real event: Brad Dalke, Wesley Bryan, Peter Finch and Ryan Ruffels. AI captains mostly take the best available handicap but sometimes reach for someone else, so no two drafts are the same. When the draft is done, every team plays every other team once and the top two meet in the final. Better teams win more often, but upsets happen, just like on the course.

Fan-made simulation, not affiliated with Barstool Sports, Bob Does Sports or the Internet Invitational. Handicap estimates by YT Golf Stats.
