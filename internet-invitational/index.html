The Internet Invitational is a team golf tournament for YouTube golfers, creators and famous athletes, run by Barstool Sports and Bob Does Sports with Dave Portnoy as host. Season 2 grew from 48 to 64 players, split into four teams of 16 led by Brad Dalke, Wesley Bryan, Peter Finch and Ryan Ruffels. The purse rose from $1 million in 2025 to at least $4 million. The episodes were filmed at Big Cedar Lodge in Missouri in August.

Episode schedule and where to watch

Season 1 in short

The first Internet Invitational in 2025 had 48 players in two teams, captained by Bob Does Sports' Robby Berger and Fore Play's Riggs, and a $1 million prize. Brad Dalke, Francis Ellis and Cody "Beef" Franke won it. Dalke and Ellis are back as defending champions, now on different teams.
