How Dailygolf Tour works

Design your own golfer, name them and fill your bag with up to 14 clubs. Swing with three taps: start, power (the white line is the distance you picked) and accuracy (the light zone hits it straight). Drag on the course to aim and to pick your landing spot.

Daily hole: one new hole a day, the same for everyone. Weekly tour: a new event every week, four rounds of nine holes from Thursday to Sunday with a cut after round two. Career: a 20-event season with four majors at your own pace.

The courses go from Florida palms and Arizona desert to English links, heathland, mountains and island coasts. Every course and player is made up: no real brands or tours.
